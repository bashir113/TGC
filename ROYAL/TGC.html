ABOUT TGC

A Legacy of Academic Academic
Excellence

The Green Collage Jos is a premier private school dedicated to nurturing the
intellectual, moral, and social development of every student . Since our founding, we
have built a proud tradition of excellence in science, mathematics, and the humanities.

Our expeirenced facuity, modern facilities, and holistic approach ensure that every
TGC graduate is confident, knowledgeable, and ready for the future.

Our Mission

To provide world-class education rooted in science and
values, producing graduates who are intellectually capable
and morally upright.

Our Vision
